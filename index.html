Biography

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Princeton AI Lab (AI2), working under the mentorship of Prof. Mengdi Wang and Prof. Le Cong. My research focuses on AI for Science and Trustworthy AI. Prior to this, I completed my PhD at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was advised by Prof. Qi Liu. During 2023-2024, I was co-advised by Prof. Marinka Zitnik from Harvard University. In 2019, I obtained my bachelor's degree from the School of the Gifted Young at USTC, majoring in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics. In 2021, I was a research intern at Tencent, mentored by Dr. Chee-Kong Lee. I have had the privilege of collaborating with Prof. Neil Gong, Prof. Earl Dowell, and Prof. Marinka Zitnik.

Research Statement in One Sentence: I build AI agents/Foundation Models/World Models to accelerate science discovery while ensuring safety and trustworthiness.

Featured Workshop Organization

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop: Biosecurity Safeguards for Generative AI
I am proud to serve as a core organizer of this pioneering workshop that addresses the critical intersection of AI innovation and biosecurity challenges. This workshop brings together leading experts in AI, synthetic biology, biosecurity policy, and ethics to develop actionable solutions for responsible AI development in life sciences.
Website: https://biosafe-gen-ai.github.io/
Date: December 2025, San Diego, California, USA
Mission: Engineering next-generation technical guardrails, establishing risk prediction benchmarks, and building a global coalition for secure AI innovation in biotechnology.
